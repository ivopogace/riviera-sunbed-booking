Riviera for beach venues

Let tourists pre-book your sunbeds online, on a map of your own beach.

What your venue gets

  • Online pre-booking on your own beach map, drawn the way your beach is laid out.
  • A daily view of your beach: mark walk-in guests, and check guests in by scanning the QR code on their booking.
  • Request-to-book, if you want to approve each booking before the guest pays.

What it costs

Free until 31 October 2028. No commission, no setup fee, no fee on walk-ins.

After that, a flat seasonal subscription. We will announce the price at least 3 months ahead, and we will never take a cut of your bookings.

How guests pay you

At launch

Guests pay you directly, by card, into your own payment account. Riviera never holds your money, and there is nothing to pay out.

How to start

  1. Register a venue account.
  2. Wait for us to approve it.
  3. Draw your beach map.
  4. Set your prices.
