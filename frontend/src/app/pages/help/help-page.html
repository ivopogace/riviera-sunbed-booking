How booking works

  • Pick a beach venue on the map, choose your day or days and a sunbed set (2 loungers + umbrella, for the full day), then pay in the app.
  • Some venues confirm instantly; others review your request first and then ask you to pay. Your booking is confirmed once payment is confirmed.
  • You get a booking code on screen and by email. Keep it private: whoever has the code can open the booking.

Cancelling

Open your booking with its code (Find a booking in the menu, or the link in your email). It shows whether you can still cancel and the refund you would get; the terms you saw before paying apply.

At the beach

  • Show your booking code, or its QR code, to the venue staff when you arrive.
  • If your set is taken or the venue is closed, ask the staff first. If that doesn't sort it out, contact us with your booking code.
