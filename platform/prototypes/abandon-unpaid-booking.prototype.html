Can a guest give up an unpaid booking?

Today a tourist who reaches the payment page and changes their mind has no way out. The set stays held until a background sweep reclaims it — about twenty minutes after an instant booking, about twelve hours after a venue accepts a request. This demo adds one new guest action, “Give up this booking”, and lets you push it through the cases that are hard to reason about on paper.

The question: is it safe to let the guest release their own hold — and what should they be told when the payment is already in flight, already through, or the card provider can’t be reached?
