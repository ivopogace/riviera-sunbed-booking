Riviera Sunbed Booking — Individual Contributor Assignment Agreement, version 1.0
(5 September 2026).

This agreement is between **Ivo Pogace** (the "Owner") and you, the person named in the
acceptance block at the end (the "Contributor"). It applies to everything you contribute
to the Riviera Sunbed Booking repository and to any related work you hand the Owner.

this agreement governs for your contributions.

**Read it before you contribute anything.** By accepting it you give up all rights in
your contributions, including the right to reuse the code you write. If you do not
agree, do not contribute.


## 1. Definitions

**"Contribution"** means any work of authorship you submit to the Owner for inclusion in

scripts, documentation, designs, images, review suggestions, issue attachments,
messages, or anything else, whether submitted as a pull request, commit, patch, issue,
comment, message, or otherwise, now or in the future.

**"Software"** means the Riviera Sunbed Booking repository and everything in it, in every
version and branch, as defined in section 1 of the `LICENSE`.

**"Rights"** means every copyright, database right, design right, patent right, trade
secret, and other intellectual-property right, worldwide, including every application,
registration, renewal, and extension, and the right to sue for past infringement.

You irrevocably assign and transfer to the Owner, at the moment each Contribution is
submitted and without further consideration, all right, title, and interest in and to

Contribution becomes part of the Software and is owned solely by the Owner, who may use,
modify, combine, license, sell, publish, or withhold it in any way, in any medium now
known or later devised, without restriction and without accounting to you.
